RESEARCH PROTOTYPE 02

How can a price show the system behind it?

RESEARCH QUESTION

How can interactive data visualization reveal the relationships, delays, and uncertainties hidden behind an everyday price?

PERSPECTIVE

Treat the retail price as a visible surface, then look backward through the market stages, delays, and evidence that give it form.

PROPOSITION

An everyday price is not a single fact. It is a visible surface shaped by systems behind it.

METHOD

Relation arranges records from backstage supply to storefront price. Chronology turns February, March, and April into three adjacent windows while keeping wholesale and retail definitions distinct.

CAUSAL CAUTION

Connections preserve qualified language from the sources: coincides with, contributed to, may delay. Sequence is not presented as proof.

BOUNDARY

The first study window covers February–April 2025. This is an evidence interface, not a retailer-margin calculator or complete causal model.
