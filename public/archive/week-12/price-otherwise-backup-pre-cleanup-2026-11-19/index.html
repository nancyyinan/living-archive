RESEARCH PROTOTYPE 02

How can a price show the system behind it?

This interface treats price as a research object rather than a single number. Relation shows evidence as a spatial field. Chronology aligns two unlike price series while keeping their definitions visible.

COMPARISON

Terracotta records are New York wholesale benchmarks for large eggs. Blue records are BLS U.S. city average retail prices for Grade A large eggs. They differ in geography, market stage, and product definition.

CAUSAL CAUTION

Connections preserve qualified language from the sources: coincides with, contributed to, may delay. Sequence is not presented as proof.

BOUNDARY

The first study window covers February–April 2025. The system is an evidence interface, not a retailer-margin calculator or complete causal model.
