# Price, Otherwise — Editorial Atlas demo

## 3–4 minute route

1. Begin in **Relation**. Explain the two layers: the upper field groups evidence by market role; the lower band places the same records in time.
2. Click **HPAI depopulation**. Its point, timeline projection, and documented relation to layer inventory become legible while the full evidence opens on demand.
3. Follow **reduces → Table-egg layer inventory**, then **constrains → Supply pressure**. Emphasize that the displayed verbs come from the research dataset.
4. Click **February wholesale** and switch to **Chronology**. The selected record and evidence panel persist.
5. Compare the straight terracotta and blue segments. Wholesale falls from February to March while retail rises; monthly observations are not smoothed into invented daily data.
6. Close the evidence drawer and open **Index**. Search “March,” then filter to “Retail.”
7. Open **Sources** to show the USDA ERS/AMS, BLS/FRED, and CRS evidence library.

## Reading the composition

- Upper X position = market role: supply, mechanism, wholesale, retail.
- Upper Y position = separation of related records within each role; it is not a numerical axis.
- Lower X position = documented month or study-window context.
- Lower lanes = visual separation of categories, not magnitude.
- Curves from upper to lower = the same evidence record projected into chronology.
- Curves between upper points = only relationships in data.js.
- Chronology Y position = price in USD per dozen for the six price observations.

## Key language

- Wholesale: New York monthly benchmark for large eggs, USDA ERS using AMS data.
- Retail: BLS U.S. city average for Grade A large eggs, not seasonally adjusted.
- The series differ in geography, market stage, product mix, and methodology.
- Sequence and correlation are not presented as proof of causation.
