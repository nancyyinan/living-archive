# Comparison with the existing prototype

## Preserved

- The complete 11-record dataset and six-source library.
- Every verified value, definition, limitation, relation verb, and evidence URL.
- Relation and Chronology modes.
- Selection context across views.
- Index, search, category filters, source library, and on-demand evidence detail.

## Changed in this independent study

| Existing prototype | Editorial atlas study |
| --- | --- |
| Dark dashboard field | Warm ivory editorial canvas |
| Rectangular information cards | Small points with short labels |
| One spatial network | Upper relationship layer plus lower chronology |
| Card boundaries carry hierarchy | Position, color, line weight, and whitespace carry hierarchy |
| Detail competes with the field | Detail remains fully hidden until selection |
| Chronology is a row of cards | Chronology uses a price axis and straight segments between observed months |

## Coordinate logic

No geographic coordinates are used. Upper positions encode market role and separate related records. Lower positions encode documented dates, month groupings, and category lanes. Projection curves connect two representations of the same record; relation curves are generated only from the existing relations array.

This directory is independent from ../price-otherwise/ and can be reviewed without replacing it.
