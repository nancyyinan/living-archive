    PROTOTYPE QUESTION

    How does an everyday price acquire meaning through the records around it?

    An everyday price is not an isolated number. It is a visible moment within several interconnected economic histories.

    TWO READINGS OF ONE ARCHIVE

    Chronology asks when the records appeared. Relation asks how supply events, wholesale observations, retail observations, and documented market mechanisms might be connected.

    EVIDENCE STATUS

    Solid connections encode mechanisms described by USDA. Dashed connections encode observed temporal sequence. Sequence does not prove causality. No unverified hypothesis is encoded as a connection in this collection.

    COMPARABILITY LIMIT

    The New York wholesale benchmark and BLS U.S. city-average retail series differ in geography, product scope, and collection method. Their gap is not a retailer-margin calculation.
