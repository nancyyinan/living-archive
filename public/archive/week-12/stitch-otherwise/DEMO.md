# Stitch, Otherwise — visual study demo

## 2–3 minute route

1. Begin in Relation and state that the screen is a data template, not a result.
2. Point to the central 24-X action grid and the planned sequence beneath it.
3. Select X01. Show how one stitch record would link to Material, Time, and Correction fields.
4. Open the detail drawer. Every unmeasured field says “Not recorded”; null is not shown as zero.
5. Switch to Chronology while X01 remains selected. The real completion-time, active-duration, and correction lanes are intentionally empty.
6. Open Schema to show the 17 proposed fields and their definitions.
7. End with the research test: after 20–30 measured stitches, do duration, interruption, material, and rework create a meaningful visual rhythm?

## What this prototype demonstrates

- A completed X can function as the smallest data unit.
- Repetition can create spatial rhythm without decorative embroidery imagery.
- The atlas can distinguish planned structure from observed evidence.
- The design has room for actual sequence and measured variation without inventing values now.
