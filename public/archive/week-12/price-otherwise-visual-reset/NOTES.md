# Price, Otherwise — Visual Reset

Independent visual study. This folder does not replace either `price-otherwise` or `price-otherwise-kinetic-atlas`.

## Current study

- One shared, zero-to-nine-dollar vertical scale.
- Three reported monthly observations only: February, March, and April 2025.
- Straight segments connect observations; no daily values are implied.
- Terracotta: New York wholesale benchmark.
- Muted blue: U.S. urban average retail price.
- Default selection: March, so the February-to-March divergence is immediately visible.
- Evidence and comparison limits open on demand.
- Relation mode keeps the price chart as its spatial reference and shows one traceable relationship category at a time: observed temporal relationship, documented mechanism, or unverified hypothesis.

## Demonstration

1. Begin at March and point to the opposing February-to-March slopes.
2. Drag the scrubber to compare exact values for each month.
3. Open **Evidence** to show that geography, product definition, and market stage are not treated as identical.
4. Switch to **Relations** and compare the three evidence statuses without resetting the selected month.
