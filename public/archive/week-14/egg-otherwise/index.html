Research question

How can participatory data visualization help people construct an understanding of the hidden systems behind an everyday object?

Interaction rule

Data determines which connections are available. The visitor determines which supported paths to pursue.

Spatial rule

Anchors are arranged by thematic sector, process stage, and—where applicable—chronological order. Their positions are not measured quantities.

Evidence rule

Observed comparisons, documented mechanisms, shared contexts, and unsupported pairs remain visually and textually distinct.
